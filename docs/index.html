From camera frame to saved encounter

The Nicla runs the full processing path. The localhost dashboard shows the frame, detector box, crop, model output, and timing linked by boot ID, frame ID, and JPEG hash.

Working prototype

Live inference, video with inference, one photo per confirmed encounter, bounded on-board photo storage, and duplicate-safe USB import are implemented. The gallery supports explicit manual filing under Person 1–5 and Other.

Current scope

R19 runs identity inference with known-person acceptance disabled. The compact recognition models had limited acceptance on held-out crops, so the final study follows the original proposal's adaptive face-detection fallback.

On-board storage and import passed with USB power and a detached host serial connection. A physical power-bank boot rehearsal remains pending. Photo saving was disabled during the power comparison.

What the study covers

Four workloads separate camera capture, detector work, and the two full pipelines. Ten accepted starts give 40 qualified measurement windows, with the order of fixed and gated inference balanced across starts.

We also report ten-seed host recognition benchmarks and 13 compression arms. Those experiments explain the model choice and its limitations.

Read the experimental design

What remains open

The main result describes one board, one meter, and one repeated optical workload. The FNIRSI meter had no independent calibration. Broader camera conditions, reliable known-person recognition, and battery runtime need dedicated validation.
