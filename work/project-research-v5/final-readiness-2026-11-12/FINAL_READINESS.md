Across ten independently power-cycled starts with seeds 62001–62007, 62009, 62010, and 62012, scene gating saved **49.54 mW (7.28%)** on the same 29-second workload. The paired 95% t interval was **46.97–52.12 mW saved**, and all ten differences favored scene gating. Both modes use the same configured capture interval and small model. Scene gating skips detector and downstream work while the scene is quiet and periodically checks again. Detector calls fell from 498 to 160, identity runs from 158 to 56, and quiet detections from four to one. Both modes matched the same three face segments in every block and missed the far-outdoor segment in every block. All 214 identity inferences completed, with acceptance of known people disabled. The latency median covers matched episodes only.

The ten starts used distinct board boot IDs and meter runs. Fixed inference ran first in five accepted blocks. The raw replay reproduced all 40 windows within `4.0e-9 W` of the saved power estimates. The ledger accounts for 28 rejected attempts, including geometry, serial, and out-of-range meter readings. Powered USB resets are separate diagnostics. The interval describes repeatability on this setup with one meter, one board, and one repeated clip. There was no independent meter calibration or battery runtime measurement. Keep the bench power as the primary result.
